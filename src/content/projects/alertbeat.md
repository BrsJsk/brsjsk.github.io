A real-time vehicle and asset tracking platform used in Greece, built around OBD and hardwired
GPS trackers with an embedded pan-European SIM. Unlike most trackers on the market, the hardware
is a one-time purchase with no ongoing monthly subscription.

The mobile apps cover real-time tracking, anti-theft security, and commercial fleet and logistics
management, and ship on both Google Play and the App Store.
