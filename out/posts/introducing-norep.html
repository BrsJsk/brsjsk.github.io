Introducing NoRep

August 8, 2025

Most fitness apps are bloated.

Popups, onboarding wizards, friend lists, paywalls, subscription screens — all while you're just trying to log your sets.

I built NoRep after getting fed up with gym tracker apps that slowed me down. I just wanted to track my workouts — but instead, I kept running into paywalls, popups, and forced social sharing.

So I made something better.

NoRep is a fast, minimal workout tracker designed for people who want to lift, log, and move on.
It runs in your browser, works on mobile and desktop, and gives you everything you need to track your gym sessions — without the fluff.

What NoRep Offers

  • Routine templates to build and reuse your workouts
  • Workout history and stats to track progress over time
  • Privacy-first design — your data stays with you
  • Works on any device, straight from your browser
  • Completely free — no subscriptions, trials, or upsells
  • No social features — no feeds, messages, or distractions
  • A minimal, fast interface that gets out of your way

Whether you're following a structured program or just logging sessions as you go, NoRep keeps the process simple and focused.

Why NoRep?

Most gym-goers don’t need an app that nags, notifies, or pushes them into a community.

They just want to keep track of what they lifted and when — without being interrupted or marketed to.

NoRep is built for focused, independent training. It won’t try to motivate you with badges or followers. It’s a fast, reliable logbook that works anywhere you train.

Coming Soon

This is just the start. Planned features on the roadmap include:

  • Offline support
  • Custom themes and UI preferences
  • Advanced workout stats and analysis
  • Deeper routine customization tools

Try It Soon

NoRep will be launching shortly. If you're looking for a clean, private, and distraction-free gym tracker, it's almost here.
